## Communication

### Band Calendar

The band calendar is the official source of truth for band events.

Subscribe to the calendar instead of downloading a one-time copy. Subscribing allows updates to appear automatically.

[Open the calendar and subscription options](/calendar).

### Family Portal

Every student should be connected to at least one parent or guardian in the Ashley Bands Family Portal.

The Family Portal is used for:

- student information
- parent contact information
- financial accounts
- instrument / music / equipment records
- band communication

[Open the Family Portal](/portal).

If your email is new or does not connect to your student, request access from the portal. If you still need help, [email Mr. Parker](mailto:robert.parker@nhcs.net).

### Google Chat

Students may be added to class-specific Google Chat groups using their school-managed NHCS accounts.

Google Chat is for students only. Parents do not have access to student Google Chat groups.

Important note: with the change from Google to Microsoft, we may transition to Microsoft Teams this year.

## Student Materials

[Student Resources (The Band Folder)](/info/the-band-folder) is the main page for student supplies, clothing, and methods.

Students should have:

- instrument in working condition
- reeds, valve oil, cork grease, sticks/mallets, or other instrument-specific supplies
- pencil
- black binder or organized folder system
- music brought to class consistently

Percussionists should have a personal stick bag and required sticks/mallets as assigned.

## Concert Attire

Concert attire is musician black.

All students wear all black for concerts. No colors, logos, or patterns.

Tops: black shirt, blouse, dress shirt, or dress-appropriate top. No sleeveless, strapless, halter, or crop tops.

Bottoms: black dress pants/slacks or knee-length-or-longer skirt/dress. No jeans, leggings, yoga pants, sweatpants, or shorts.

Shoes: black dress shoes or flats. No sneakers, flip-flops, or athletic shoes.

Socks/tights: black.

## Red Band Shirt

All band members need the official red band shirt.

The red band shirt is used for pep rallies, community performances, parades, and informal events.

[Buy the official Red Band Shirt](https://ashleybandshirts.printify.me/).

## Parent Involvement

Parents are part of the success of the program.

Useful ways to help include:

- attend concerts and events
- help students keep dates on the family calendar
- encourage consistent practice
- support fundraising when possible
- volunteer for events, trips, hospitality, equipment, or booster work

All parents are connected to the Band Boosters simply by being part of the program.
